Why the graphical method is approximate: The Burmester method gives exact 3-position synthesis. With a 4th point, B₁ is from circumcenter(P₁,P₂,P₃) only, so B₄ may not lie on the rocker circle — this gap is the closure error. The Numerical tab solves all 4 simultaneously and is always exact.
